The Venue

Castello di Rosciano

Località Signoria, 77/a, 06089 Torgiano PG, Italy

From Italian Airports

Driving Directions

From Rome Fiumicino (FCO)

  1. Exit the airport via the A91 heading north towards the GRA ring road
  2. Join the Grande Raccordo Anulare (GRA) and head north
  3. Take the A1 motorway north towards Florence
  4. Exit at Orvieto and join the E45 north towards Perugia
  5. Follow signs for Torgiano — the venue is signposted on the approach

~1h 45m · ~160 km · Toll roads on the A1

From Perugia Airport (PEG)

  1. Exit the airport following signs for Perugia / E45
  2. Take the E45 south towards Torgiano
  3. Exit at Torgiano and follow signs to Castello di Rosciano

~20 min · ~15 km · The closest airport to the venue
